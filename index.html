FORM TOOL · STAGED RADIAL ENGAGEMENT (WORK Z / PREVIEW ONLY)

STOCK-AWARE REVIEW: earlier nominal OD rough/finish/groove passes contribute to an axial remaining-stock envelope. Sample the whole form edge at each X position; report estimated contact width and radial engagement, and optionally skip initial clear stages. This is model evidence only, NOT measured stock or approved cutting forces. The pre-form limit controls REVIEW status; it is not an automatic feed/speed recommendation.

FLANK-BIASED INFEED: synchronized X/Z stages offset the full form edge toward Z− or Z+, converging to the exact finished form location. The complete edge is tested against the finished profile at each stage. An unavailable or gouging bias is BLOCKED, not automatically treated as safe. The contact-width estimate is compared with a radial baseline; it does NOT prove cutting forces, flank relief, or tool mounting.

Model is an IDEAL RADIAL form edge. For RADIAL mode, the preview stages X only at a fixed Z reference. SHORT BACKOFF is default for NEW form operations (old saved modes retained); CONTINUOUS FEED stays at Z and feeds deeper without intermediate retract; SHORT BACKOFF briefly moves X outward (diameter mode uses twice the radial value); FULL CLEARANCE repeats the old return. None models chip breaking, flank relief, lateral side-feed or actual cutting force. It does NOT establish complete cutting-edge engagement, rake/relief, peak cutting force, chip control, holder clearance, a valid finish pass, or NC suitability. A wide edge can require roughing by another tool first.

SPECIAL CONTOUR · FLANK ROUGH + FINISH

WHOLE GROOVE: select BOTH shoulder points (for example P02→P04) around a single valley. Each rough pass follows the FULL groove from the front/Z+ shoulder down through the bottom and out the rear shoulder at its successive X depth. After the far shoulder, X withdraws straight outward to an outside-stock diameter, and Z returns above stock to the entrance for the next rough pass; no midpoint-first pass or reverse flank recut. The final pass starts at the FRONT shoulder and stops at the groove bottom midpoint, can optionally dwell for 0–5 spindle revolutions (PREVIEW ANNOTATION ONLY), then retracts straight outward in X. Do NOT use this for NC without proving the cutting-edge engagement, shoulder entry, tool body sweep, holder access, and return. SINGLE FLANK retains the older experimental path. Use a threading or other narrow tool on ONE selected OD contour flank (for example P02→P03, then P03→P04 in a separate operation). A rough+finish candidate uses successive X depth steps along the selected flank, leaving an editable radial finish allowance, then traces the final flank. END AT TANGENT is the conservative default. ARC MIDPOINT includes only HALF of an adjacent end fillet, never the complete opposite flank. For ARC MIDPOINT the cut travels from the selected OUTER shoulder toward the fillet midpoint, regardless of the source arc ordering. SHORT X BACKOFF feeds a small radial distance outward and retraces the preceding offset flank back to its outer entry without returning to safe-front or full X clearance between roughing passes; this retrace is NOT a verified clear return. FULL OUTSIDE RETURN preserves the former plan. The final pass withdraws to full clearance. For an F11 form tool, the tool-datum trace is derived from the selected cutting lip and the full-edge finished-surface envelope; the limiting edge may lift the cutter off the selected flank. Candidate paths still require a separately reviewed center cleanup. The same F11 custom form tool can be assigned here or to Form Radial; its whole-edge engagement is NOT certified. No stock removal or spindle synchronization is proven. An R0 thread tool uses nominal tip coordinates only (no fictional nose compensation). A 60° threading insert does NOT automatically qualify for a V-groove. NC LOCKED.
